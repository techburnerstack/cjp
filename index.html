Manifesto

Six demands for a civilization that needs a nap.

Clear, satirical, and designed to be remembered. No 200-page PDF required.

  1. 01

    Right to rest

    Every citizen deserves breaks without a moral lecture in the background.

  2. 02

    WiFi where it matters

    Public spaces should connect people, not test their patience and data packs.

  3. 03

    Jobs with honesty

    No fake internships, no exposure payments, no passion used as salary.

  4. 04

    Queue justice

    Lines should move, forms should make sense, and counters should be open.

  5. 05

    Affordable snacks

    Democracy is stronger when chai, vada pav, and instant noodles remain within reach.

  6. 06

    Less noise, more sense

    Public life should reward clarity, not the loudest microphone in the room.
